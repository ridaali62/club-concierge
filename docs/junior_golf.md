# Junior Golf

## Who can join
Junior golf is for members' children aged 8 to 17.

## Playing rules
Juniors under 12 must play with an adult member. Juniors aged 12 to 17 who pass the playing ability test may play without an adult on weekdays after 2:00 pm.

## Summer camp
The junior summer golf camp runs every week in July and costs $450 per week.

## Junior league
The junior league plays on Tuesday afternoons during the season. Sign up at the pro shop.

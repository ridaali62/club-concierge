# Member Mobile App

## Getting the app
Download the "Maple Ridge Club" app from the App Store or Google Play.

## Signing in
Sign in with your member number and the email address on your membership.

## Features
The app lets you book tee times and courts, reserve dining tables, enter the weekend lottery, view statements, register guests and see the events calendar.

## Forgotten password
Tap "Forgot password" on the sign-in screen and follow the link sent to your email.

## Notifications
Turn push notifications on in the app settings to receive weather delays, lottery results and event reminders.

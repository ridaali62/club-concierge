# Dining Hours & Reservations

## Grill Room hours
Breakfast is served from 7:00 am to 10:30 am every day. Lunch runs from 11:30 am to 3:00 pm. Dinner is served Tuesday to Sunday from 5:30 pm to 9:30 pm. The Grill Room is closed for dinner on Mondays.

## The Terrace
The Terrace is open from May to September, 11:00 am to 8:00 pm, weather permitting. It serves a lighter menu of salads, sandwiches and grilled dishes.

## Reservations
Book tables in the member app or by calling the dining desk (ext. 220). Reservations are recommended for dinner. Parties of 8 or more need at least 48 hours' notice so the kitchen can plan.

## Food and beverage minimum
Each membership has a quarterly food and beverage minimum of $300. Any unused amount is billed on the statement at the end of the quarter.

## Dietary requests
Tell the server or add a note to your reservation. The kitchen can prepare vegetarian, gluten-free and halal dishes with advance notice.

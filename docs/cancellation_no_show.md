# Cancellation & No-Show Rules

## Cancelling a tee time
Cancel in the member app at least 24 hours before your tee time and there is no charge.

## Late cancellations
Cancelling less than 24 hours before the tee time costs $20 per player.

## No-shows
A no-show is charged $40 per player. After three no-shows in one season, the member's online booking privileges are suspended for 14 days.

## Dining cancellations
Cancel dinner reservations at least 4 hours in advance. Parties of 8 or more must cancel 48 hours in advance, otherwise a charge of $15 per person applies.

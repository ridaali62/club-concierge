# Dress Code

## On the course
Collared shirts are required for all players. Denim is not permitted on the course or the driving range. Shorts must be at least mid-thigh length. Only soft-spike golf shoes may be worn.

## In the clubhouse
Smart casual dress is expected in the clubhouse. Jackets are required for gentlemen in the Grill Room after 6:00 pm on Fridays and Saturdays. Hats must be removed indoors.

## Pool and fitness areas
Swimwear is for the pool deck only. Please wear a cover-up and footwear when walking to the Terrace.

## Mobile phones
Keep phones on silent in the clubhouse and on the course. Calls may be taken in the lobby and the parking area.

# Tennis & Pickleball

## Courts
The club has 6 clay tennis courts and 4 pickleball courts. Courts are lit until 10:00 pm.

## Booking courts
Book tennis and pickleball courts in the member app up to 3 days in advance. Singles bookings last 60 minutes and doubles bookings last 90 minutes.

## Footwear
Non-marking court shoes are required on all courts.

## Lessons
Lessons with the tennis professional cost $70 per hour.

## Open play
Pickleball open play is held every Saturday from 9:00 am to 11:00 am. No booking is needed.

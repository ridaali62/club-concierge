# Tee-Time Booking

## Booking window
Members can book tee times up to 7 days in advance. Bookings for a given day open at 7:00 am exactly seven days before.

## Weekend lottery
Saturday and Sunday tee times between 7:00 am and 11:00 am are allocated by lottery. Enter the lottery in the member app by Wednesday at 6:00 pm. Results are sent through the app on Thursday at 9:00 am.

## Group size
A tee time holds a maximum of 4 players, including guests.

## Tee-time intervals
The first tee time is 6:30 am. Tee times are spaced 10 minutes apart.

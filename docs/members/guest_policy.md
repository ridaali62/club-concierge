# Guest Policy & Guest Fees

## Bringing guests
Members may bring up to 3 guests per round of golf. The same guest may play a maximum of 6 times per calendar year.

## Weekend mornings
On Saturdays and Sundays before 11:00 am, each member may bring a maximum of two guests, and guests must play with the hosting member.

## Guest fees
The guest green fee is $85 on weekdays and $110 on weekends and public holidays. A shared cart costs $25 per guest. All guest fees are charged to the hosting member's account.

## Registering guests
Register guests in the member app at least 24 hours before the tee time. Guests check in at the pro shop before play.

## Member responsibility
The hosting member is responsible for their guests' conduct and for making sure guests follow the dress code.

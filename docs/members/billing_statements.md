# Member Billing & Statements

## Monthly statements
Statements are issued on the 1st of each month and are available in the member app.

## Payment due date
Payment is due by the 25th of the month.

## Late payments
Overdue balances are charged a late fee of 1.5% per month.

## Autopay
Members can set up automatic payment by card or bank transfer in the member app.

## Charges and disputes
Sign all charges with your member number. Questions or disputes about a charge must be raised with the accounting office (ext. 240) within 30 days of the statement date.

# Fitness Centre

## Opening hours
The fitness centre is open daily from 5:30 am to 10:00 pm.

## Age rules
Members aged 16 and over may use the fitness centre on their own. Members aged 14 and 15 must be accompanied by a parent.

## Personal training
Personal training sessions cost $75 per hour. Book at the fitness centre front desk (ext. 250).

## Classes
Yoga runs on Tuesdays and Thursdays at 7:00 am. Spin classes run on Mondays and Wednesdays at 6:00 pm. Classes are free for members.

## Etiquette
Towels are provided. Please wipe down equipment after use and limit cardio machines to 30 minutes when others are waiting.

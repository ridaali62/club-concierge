# Weather Delays & Course Closures

## Lightning and weather delays
When lightning is detected within 8 miles, the siren sounds and all players must leave the course immediately. One long siren blast means play is suspended. Three short blasts mean play may resume.

## Course closures
The golf course superintendent decides by 6:00 am whether the course opens. The course status is posted in the member app and on the course status line.

## Cart restrictions
After heavy rain, carts are restricted to the cart paths to protect the fairways.

## Rain checks
If play is suspended before a guest completes 9 holes, the guest receives a rain check valid for 60 days.

## Notifications
Members with a tee time on a delayed or closed day receive a push notification in the member app.

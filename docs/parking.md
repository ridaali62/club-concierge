# Parking

## Member parking
Members park in the main lot in front of the clubhouse. The staff lot behind the maintenance building is not for members.

## Valet
Complimentary valet parking is available on Friday and Saturday evenings from 5:00 pm to 11:00 pm.

## Electric vehicle charging
Four EV charging spaces are in the main lot, with a 2-hour limit per vehicle.

## Overnight parking
Overnight parking is not allowed without permission from the front desk.

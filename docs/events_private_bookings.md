# Events & Private Bookings

## Member events
The full member events calendar is in the member app, including holiday dinners, wine evenings and member-guest tournaments.

## Private event rooms
The Ballroom holds up to 180 guests and the Oak Room holds up to 40 guests.

## Booking a private event
Private events must be hosted by a member. Rooms can be booked up to 12 months ahead through the events office (ext. 230). A deposit of 25% of the estimated cost confirms the booking.

## Catering
The catering manager will plan the menu with you. Final guest numbers are due 7 days before the event.

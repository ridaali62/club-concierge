# Pool

## Season and hours
The outdoor pool is open from Memorial Day to Labor Day, 10:00 am to 8:00 pm. A lifeguard is on duty during all opening hours.

## Adult swim
Adult swim is held daily from 12:00 pm to 1:00 pm. Only swimmers aged 18 and over may use the pool during this time.

## Children
Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult aged 16 or older at all times.

## Pool rules
Glass containers are not allowed on the pool deck. Food from the Terrace may be eaten at the pool-side tables only.

## Guests at the pool
Pool guests pay $10 per visit, charged to the hosting member's account.
